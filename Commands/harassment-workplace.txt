# Workplace Harassment Complaint

Bayaan-e-mudda'iya: Main {complainant_name}, employee at {company_name}, ye report darj karwana chahti hoon ke mera senior officer {accused_name} pichlay {months} maheene se mujhe zabani aur ba'z martaba jismani taur par harass kar raha hai. Kaafi bar refuse karne ke bawajood us ka rawaiya jari hai. Email/message proofs munsalik hain. Protection Against Harassment of Women at Workplace Act 2010 ke tehat action liya jaye.
